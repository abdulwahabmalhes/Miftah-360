MIFTAH 360 — BUSINESS RULES V1.2

PRODUCT OWNER DECISION REGISTER



Status: APPROVED FOR IMPLEMENTATION

Date: 30 October 2026

Purpose: Supersedes conflicting assumptions/open questions in V1.1 and existing PRDs. All unchanged V1.1 rules remain valid.



PO-001 Authentication

Car Owner: Email+Password, Phone+OTP, Google Sign-In. Account linking prevents duplicate users. International phone architecture.

Workshop Owner/Staff: Email+Password, Phone+OTP, no Google.

Miftah Admin: Email+Password + mandatory MFA/OTP; no Google; no phone-only login.



PO-002 Vehicle required fields

Required: Brand, Model, Year, Mileage.

Optional: Trim, Fuel Type, Transmission, VIN, Plate Number, Emirate, Registration Expiry, Insurance Expiry, Photo, Notes.



PO-003 Service Request recipient

One request is sent to ONE selected workshop. No broadcast/auction/multi-workshop RFQ in Phase 1.



PO-004 Booking

Time Proposed and No Show are official states. A different proposed time requires user acceptance before confirmation.



PO-005 Preliminary Quote

Inspection is not universally required before Quote. Priceable services may have preliminary quote. Complex/diagnostic services require inspection before final authorization price. Material post-inspection change requires new Quote revision + new user approval.



PO-006 Job creation

Job Card may be created when vehicle arrives/inspection begins. It is not authorized for paid execution until user accepts the applicable Quote revision.



PO-007 Invoice and Payment

Invoice Status: Draft / Issued / Cancelled.

Payment Status: Unpaid / Paid.

Paid Outside Miftah is settlement/source metadata, not Invoice status.



PO-008 Module control

Only Miftah Admin may enable/disable workshop modules, bundles and Marketplace Pack. Workshop Owner/staff cannot toggle modules.



PO-009 Workshop staff management

Workshop Owner may add/invite/deactivate staff and change fixed roles inside own workshop without Admin approval.



PO-010 Fixed Phase 1 workshop roles

Workshop Owner, Service Advisor, Reception, Technician. No custom role builder or per-user custom permission toggles in Phase 1.



PO-011 Workshop brands

Workshop selects supported Brands or All Brands. Brand compatibility is a matching eligibility condition.



PO-012 Workshop services

Workshop selects from central Miftah Service Taxonomy only. No workshop-created canonical services in Phase 1.



PO-013 Starting prices

Workshop may configure Starting From price per service. It is informative, not binding. Final authorized price comes from accepted Quote revision.



PO-014 Matching ranking

Use Best Match, not nearest-only. Hard eligibility filters first. Ranking factors include service match, brand, distance within same Emirate, rating, availability, response performance and starting price when relevant. No paid boost in Phase 1.



PO-015 Emirate scope

Matching is constrained to selected/current service Emirate. Dubai and Sharjah only in Phase 1. No automatic cross-emirate fallback. User may manually change Emirate if no results.



PO-016 Service zones

No area/service-zone configuration inside Emirate in Phase 1. One primary workshop location; distance affects ranking.



PO-017 Multi-branch

Out of Phase 1. Each Workshop Tenant is one operating workshop with one primary location/calendar/workspace.



PO-018 Workshop lifecycle

Pending Verification / Active / Suspended / Inactive / Rejected.

Approved is an action; approval moves workshop to Active.



PO-019 Control boundary

Miftah Admin controls activation, Marketplace participation, subscription/entitlements and modules.

Workshop Owner controls internal staff and operations only.



Binding retained rules:

- User is sole Quote approver.

- Sent Quote revision is immutable.

- Material change requires new revision + approval.

- Review only after Completed Job.

- Suspension/disable never erases history.

- Core cannot be disabled.

- Marketplace Pack must be complete for participating workshop.

- Matching Engine separate from LLM.

- AI never chooses Workshop ID.

- Payment gateway is outside Phase 1.



END.
